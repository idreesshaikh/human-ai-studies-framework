# Enter the protocol details directly

If you already know the design, skip the chat: one form states the whole study at
once and compiles it into the protocol draft. It works without a model, and the
chat stays available afterwards to refine what you entered.

## Open the form

- On a new study, choose **Enter protocol manually** below the chat box on
  the Setup screen.
- On a study with a draft, choose **Edit details** on the **Protocol draft**
  panel. The form starts from the current draft.
- Or open **Research tools** (the **⋯** button above the chat) and choose
  **Enter protocol manually**.

## Fill it in

The **Enter protocol details** form has five parts. Required fields are marked.

| Part | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Study** | **Study name**; **Research question 1** and up to five more, added with **Add research question** |
| **Design** | **Study design**, **Within-subjects** (each developer does both conditions, in counterbalanced order) or **Between-subjects** (each does one); **Condition 1** and **Condition 2** |
| **Participants** | **Who takes part** (role, experience, inclusion criteria); **Planned participants**, 4 to 1000, or 6 to 1000 with at least 3 per condition when between-subjects; **Session length**, 15 to 180 minutes |
| **Task** | **What participants do** |
| **Outcomes** | Up to six outcomes, ticked from the supported measures, such as time to the first passing test, whether the tests pass, workload (raw TLX) and self-rated code understanding. **Other outcomes** matches what you type against the measure catalogue and offers an **Add** button for each match |

An outcome with no supported analysis for the chosen design is greyed out and
says so. A custom outcome that the catalogue does not cover cannot be saved here:
set up its capture and analysis in the [design conversation](design-conversation.md).

Choose **Save draft**. If something is missing or out of range, the form lists
each problem and links to its field.

## What saving does

Saving records your entry as accepted decisions that replace the earlier ones, and
compiles them into the **Protocol draft** panel. It keeps the analysis recipes of
research questions you did not change. Nothing reaches a participant yet: choose
**Review draft** to check the compiled protocol, then **Apply protocol**.
