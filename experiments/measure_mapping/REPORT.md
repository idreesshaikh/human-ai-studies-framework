# Measure mapping: implementation and draft-label status

No empirical model comparison has been performed. Under the owner's delegated
implementation authority, the product offers deterministic catalog-alias proposals
with human confirmation and `calibrated=false`. No trained decision model is
selected or claimed to be validated. Independent annotation and preregistration
remain prerequisites for empirical comparison.

At the owner's request, the proposed 20-class catalog now has model-authored
descriptions, supported source paths, design-specific recipe pointers and example
phrases. Its digest identifies a draft revision; it does not freeze an approved
label space. Recipe pointers are proposed mappings, not methodological validation.

There are 56 repository seed phrases from 19 templates and four instruments.
Each records the source location, model label provenance, reason, review confidence
and `adjudicated=false`. All are training only. Twelve generic or unsupported
phrases are labeled `none` and listed for review. E6 and E10 have no repository
seed examples in this extraction. Repeated SUS items are one source group, not ten
independent evaluations. The catalog's illustrative phrases are also model-authored.

Generic metrics, baselines, sealed split/evaluation workflows, blind-sheet generation
and agreement reports are checked with synthetic tests. Mutations confirm that splitting
individual items instead of source groups and silently reading test labels are
detected. Equal-confidence answers stay together. Single-label kappa is undefined;
degenerate bootstrap statistics cannot produce an interval. These checks validate
software behavior, not model accuracy, label quality or researcher usefulness.

Human source phrases: not collected. Independent annotations: not collected.
Kappa and empirical confidence intervals: not measured. Calibration/test split:
not created. Model comparison, baseline table and deployment threshold: not measured.
The generic keyword model has only synthetic characterization tests.

The next scientific steps are catalog review, independent collection and annotation,
adjudication, signed preregistration, source-disjoint splitting and a recorded test
lock. If fewer than 100 independent real test items are available, report only
descriptive results. Model-assisted labels cannot serve as independent test truth.
No model weights or workspace ML dependencies were added. The product catalog and
suggestion endpoints support typed v6 authoring, without reporting classification
probabilities or empirical usefulness. Existing scales, IDs, wording and covariates
are preserved; unsupported or unresolved outcomes cannot silently downgrade capture.
