# Finish the project

The current delivery scope is tool cleanup, consolidation of the existing
interview study, a clear example demonstration, and a final report that can be
adapted into a workshop paper. A separate effectiveness experiment, self-study,
large benchmark or new model is not part of this delivery.

## Deliverables

| Deliverable | Material | Completion boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Working tool | [Demo verification](demo-readiness.md) | Required checks pass; presentation environment is rehearsed |
| Example demonstration | [Showcase walkthrough](demo-walkthrough.md) | Explain the complete workflow and its limits in seven minutes |
| Interview consolidation | [Interview guide](interview-study.md) | Existing sources are inventoried and findings trace back to them |
| Additional interviews | [Eight-hour budget](interview-study.md#eight-hour-budget) | Two or three interviews and their analysis stay within the time box |
| Final report | [Paper-shaped draft](project-report-draft.md) | Insert verified interview findings and complete author/source review |
| Submission decision | [WSESE guidance](wsese-2027-readiness.md) | Supervisor reviews the contribution, category and actual evidence |

## Work in order

1. Rehearse the installed build and synthetic example. Fix observed failures;
   preserve existing study records and the current concise interface.
2. Inventory existing interview material before adding questions or codes.
   Store participant identifiers and sources in approved private storage.
3. Conduct at most three additional interviews, eliciting actual study practice
   before showing the tool. Keep demo reactions separate from descriptions of
   existing needs.
4. Consolidate findings using the original analytic approach. Retain
   counterexamples and uncertainty; do not invent prevalence, agreement or
   saturation claims.
5. Complete the report's interview section and verify every empirical claim
   against the source register. The synthetic demo illustrates the artifact;
   it contributes no participant findings.

## Current evidence and outstanding inputs

The repository provides code, tests, architecture, a populated synthetic
example and software verification records. Interview transcripts, the original
interview guide, consent records and an existing coding table have not yet
been located in this workspace. Their absence here is an input gap, not
evidence that the interviews did not happen.

Before final submission the team must supply the actual interview sources,
methods, dates, recruitment and sample description; confirm approved use of
quotations; and review individual contributions and the institutional rubric.
Until then, the report remains a draft with an explicitly incomplete empirical
section. Preparation materials do not count as completed interviews or analysis.

## Scope discipline

Keep [scope](scope.md) as the implementation boundary. A paper can describe a
useful, carefully engineered artifact informed by interviews without asserting
that it improves research validity. Preserve the stronger evaluations in
[research evaluation](research-evaluation.md) as future possibilities rather
than prerequisites for finishing this project.
